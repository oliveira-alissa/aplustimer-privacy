A+Timer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

A+Timer provides a customizable floating countdown and count-up timer for meetings, lessons, and events. This policy explains how the extension handles your information.

Information stored locally

A+Timer stores the following in your browser profile:

  • Clock appearance, position, size, sound, and visual-effect preferences.
  • Saved timer names and durations.
  • Active timer state and scheduled completion time.
  • The latest completion and alert-delivery status, including relevant error messages.
  • An optional background image and alert sound you select, with their filenames, file types, and related local save metadata.

These records support timer operation, customization, state restoration, and error reporting. They are not sent to the developer's servers. A+Timer does not use Chrome's sync storage to synchronize them between devices.

Files and settings

File upload and removal controls open in an extension-owned page, separate from websites. A+Timer accesses only files you choose; it does not scan your other files. Selected images are used as timer backgrounds, and selected audio is used for alert playback.

Website access

Chrome may show a warning such as “Read and change all your data on websites you visit” because A+Timer requests permission to run its content scripts across supported websites. This is a broad technical capability: where access is granted, the extension can read and modify webpage content. The permission itself is not limited to timer controls.

A+Timer’s current implementation uses website access to add, position, resize, and operate the floating timer, and to keep it available as you browse. It does not use that capability to record browsing history, collect passwords or form entries, scrape page content, change search results, or inject advertising.

You can limit website access through Chrome’s extension Site access settings. The timer may not appear automatically on sites where access is withheld; refresh affected tabs after changing access. Turning the timer overlay off hides its interface but does not revoke Chrome’s website-access permission. Use Chrome’s extension settings to restrict access or disable the extension.

Content deliberately displayed in the floating timer, such as a custom image or timer name, appears within the visited webpage. The page controls its own environment and may be able to observe or interfere with displayed content. Extension-owned file storage remains separate from website storage. Websites are not granted access to the extension's settings or uploaded audio through the in-page timer.

Permissions

  • Storage: saves local preferences, presets, timer state, and operational status.
  • Alarms: schedules timer completion independently of individual website tabs.
  • Offscreen audio: plays the selected alert through a hidden extension document while website tabs are hidden or closed.
  • Notifications: displays timer-completion notifications.
  • Website access: grants the ability to read and modify supported webpages; A+Timer uses it to display and operate the floating timer as described above.

Chrome and your operating system control notification presentation. The extension cannot play an alert while Chrome is fully closed or the device is asleep. Overdue completion is handled when Chrome resumes, subject to browser scheduling.

Data transmission, analytics, and advertising

A+Timer does not require an account. Its normal operation does not send stored preferences, timer records, or selected files to external servers. It does not include analytics, tracking services, advertising, or remotely hosted executable scripts.

A+Timer does not sell your information or use it for advertising, unrelated profiling, creditworthiness, or lending decisions. It does not intentionally collect passwords, payment details, location, personal communications, or browsing history. The in-page display described above remains subject to the visited website's environment.

Retention and deletion

Local preferences and files remain until you change, replace, or remove them, clear the relevant extension data, or uninstall A+Timer. Browser storage management may also affect locally stored records. Recent operational records are overwritten as new events occur rather than maintained as a usage-history log.

Use the image and sound Remove controls in A+Timer settings to delete selected files. If removal fails, an error is shown and the selection remains available for retry. Selecting a replacement overwrites the corresponding stored file. Uninstalling A+Timer removes its extension-owned local data. Clearing browsing history alone does not reliably remove extension data.

Earlier versions may have stored files in individual websites' storage. Those older copies may remain after uninstalling A+Timer. Remove them by clearing the corresponding website's site data. Older files may need to be selected again after an upgrade.

Support email

If you email support, your email provider delivers the message, email address, and attachments you choose to send. That information is used to respond to your request and is separate from the extension's local storage. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. You may contact support about a previous support message or request deletion of correspondence held by the developer, subject to any applicable retention obligations.
